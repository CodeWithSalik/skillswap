# Decision Points — SkillSwap

## DP1 · Rejection

**What can a client see and do after a creator declines?**

When a creator declines a booking, the client can still see the booking in their "My Bookings" page with a clear "Declined" status badge. The client can then choose to book a different gig from the marketplace. We chose transparency over hiding declined bookings because it helps the client understand what happened and take their next action — confusion from a silently disappearing booking would be a worse user experience.

## DP2 · Double Booking

**Can a gig accept a new booking while another is still Pending?**

Yes. A gig can receive multiple bookings even while others are in Pending status. Creators in the gig economy typically handle multiple clients and inquiries simultaneously. Blocking new bookings because one request is pending would reduce marketplace activity and could cost the creator income if the pending client is unresponsive. The creator retains full control to accept or decline each booking individually.

## DP3 · Discovery

**How are gigs ranked on the marketplace page?**

Gigs are displayed newest-first by default, combined with keyword search and category filtering. Newest-first gives recently posted gigs immediate visibility, encouraging creators to keep their listings fresh. This ranking is simple, fair, and transparent — every new creator gets a turn at the top. The search and filter tools let clients cut through volume to find exactly the skill category they need.
